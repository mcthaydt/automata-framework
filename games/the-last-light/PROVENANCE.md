# Provenance

Everything in this folder is MIT, by the Owner's word of 2026-11-11, and made here unless a section says otherwise.

## What was made here

The package, its code, tests, content files and editors' rows, written new on the framework.

## What came from the game's own repository as content

The story, the puzzle, the creature's behaviour, the cutscene scripts and the scene's layout: the Owner's own work, read
from the old game's prologue at its commit `92e6704` and carried through the framework's October fixtures (task 11 of
the plan). The English words are byte-equal to that record.

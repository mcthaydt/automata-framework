# One example set: a single place and a single cast

The Owner, 2026-11-13, on the examples: "We are redo'ing the examples correct? the existing ones look like shit"; "the 3D
examples should be a unique set, a single environment, a single set of characters"; and, when an audit began grading
the old ones: "stop the examples" and "no need to grade work we're throwing away".

So the old examples are not judged, polished or ported. They are replaced. This spec says by what.

## What done means

A stranger who opens the examples sees one small world, drawn to the same bar as the reference game, and each example is
one thing the framework does, shown in that world by the same few people. Nothing in the set is borrowed from The Last
Light; it has its own place and its own cast. Every file in it is content an editor can open, so the set is the editors'
second stress test beside the game.

## The 3D set

**One environment.** A small country railway station at the end of the line (the coordinator's proposal, open to the
Owner's correction; any place works that has these kinds of spot). It is made once, as one level with zones, each a
natural home for a capability:

- the platform: outdoors, sky, time of day, the camera turning (the opt-in yaw the game uses);
- the waiting room: props, seats, lamps, things to pick up and look at;
- the ticket office: a person behind a counter, dialogue, a small story;
- the signal box: levers and lamps as a puzzle, rules and facts;
- the store room: a locked door, a key, a dark room and a light carried in.

Surfaces are photo-made, as the world kit does them ("we should do photo texture across the board since that's our
environment builder look"); props come from the world kit; a missing photo is CC0, recorded. No asset is generated by a
service or bought.

**One cast.** Three people, each a character file with outfit, hair, gait and poses as content the Characters editor
opens: a traveller (the one you walk), the stationmaster, and a porter. They are painted at density 2 and shown crisp at
a whole-pixel scale, to the bar set for the game ("Yeah, I want the characters to look like how they do on the left").
Whatever a character needs that the content kinds cannot say today is added to the kinds and the kit for everyone,
never written as code for one person.

**The examples.** Each is the station with one thing switched on, small enough to read in a minute, and each has a page
on the site and a journey that reaches its moment. The plan makes the list from what the framework can do, not from the
old folders; it will at least cover: a level and its camera; lights and time of day; looks (the panel, the retro grade);
props and interaction; figures, clips and poses; outfits and hair; a cutscene; dialogue and a story; rules, sight and
finding the way; saving. The 3D story example is the same station played end to end.

## The 2D set

The Owner spoke of 3D. The coordinator's recommendation, for his yes or no at the end: the 2D examples follow the same
rule with Emberwick's world and people as their one set, since Emberwick is the 2D reference game and already looks the
part. Until he says, the plan covers 3D and lists 2D as a second part that does not start.

## Rules

1. **Replace, do not grade.** An old example is deleted in the commit that lands its replacement (and its site page,
   journey and recorded picture with it). No time is spent judging or fixing old ones.
2. **Data first.** The level, the cast, the poses, the cutscene, the words, the rules, the sounds and the feel are
   content files. An example's own code is the few lines that switch its one thing on.
3. **One look.** One palette, one type, one kit for prompts and buttons, across every example and its page.
4. **Phone first.** Each example is designed and tested at phone size; touch targets 44 px, primary 48; what to press is
   plain (the drunk grandma test).
5. **The set opens in every editor** and round-trips with an empty diff; a journey per editor proves it.
6. **Nothing is shown to the Owner that the coordinator has not opened** and that does not hold up; recorded pictures
   are recorded only after he has seen them.

## What it waits on

The set is built on what the launch lanes are landing: `@automata/3d` and the kits as built packages (the examples
import them as a stranger would), the editors' v2 look and the page that holds every editor, the night-and-haze fix, the
game's camera and figure-look work (the turning camera, density 2 at a whole-pixel scale, the screen mesh on texel
borders, skin that stays warm), and the scope rename. The plan says which task needs which.

## Stages

Plan (Fable 5.2, medium, writing-plans), plan audit (Opus, medium), execution (Sonnet, high), implementation audit
(Opus, medium), fixes (Sonnet, high), the Owner's review. Building the place and the cast is look work and runs on
Opus at medium effort, as the Owner ruled for work of that kind.

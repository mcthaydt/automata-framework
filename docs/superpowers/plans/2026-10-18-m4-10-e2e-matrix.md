### The matrix journey (`examples/e2e/input-matrix.e2e.ts`, create)

Per cell, on `core/core-input-rebind`, under the fake host (and standalone where the cell says so):
1. Boot → title.
2. Play.
3. Move (held right, by the cell's device). The canvas changes.
4. One action (jump).
5. Pause.
6. Settings.
7. Rebind jump to a free key: keyboard `KeyK`, pad button 3 (north), touch by pad or keyboard where the cell has one.
8. Back to pause, then resume.
9. The new binding works and the old one no longer jumps (canvas captures once the runner has settled).
10. Back to the title.

Every cell also asserts zero console errors, zero CSP violations and `checkLayout` on each screen it visits.

- **Touch:** touch rebinding is not offered (§4.5 rebinds keys and pad buttons only). A touch cell rebinds with the keyboard, the way a player with a Bluetooth keyboard would, and plays the touch buttons. The plan records this as how §9.1's "rebind one action" reads for touch.
- **Host-event cells** (desktop and phone portrait, Chromium):
  - `display.metrics` moves `hostReserved` from right to left (D-b is already proven on `core-input-touch`; here it runs on the matrix game);
  - `input.mode` set to `gamepad` changes the Settings prompts to pad glyphs;
  - backgrounding the frame (`visibilitychange` to hidden) releases a held key;
  - button 8 held reaches the fake host as `host.summon` and the game sees no action;
  - a focused text field with a simulated keyboard keeps `data-form-factor` and the screen.
- **Failure cells:**
  - the pad in use unplugged mid-play pauses with "Reconnect your controller" (M-b);
  - a non-standard pad shows its toast once;
  - with `saves` not granted, a rebind is kept for the session and the section says it was not saved.

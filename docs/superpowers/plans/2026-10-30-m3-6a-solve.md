## Decisions (open to the Owner's veto)

1. **Units.** Effector positions are figure units in their `space` (`root`: the root joint's frame, the figure's own +z forward; `chest`: the chest joint's frame), multiplied by `k = height / 1.62` at solve time. Pins and aim points are world positions (they come from the view and the sim), not scaled. Euler rotations are degrees, `(pitch, yaw, roll)`, composed as the source's `euler` (yaw · pitch · roll).
2. **Rotation handles are local.** `pelvis.rot`, `chest.rot` and `head.rot` set their joints' local rotations (as the source's `base()` writes them), so `space` does not apply to them; `pelvis.pos` is the pelvis joint's position in root space. `chest` and `head` take no position (`pos` on them is ignored; M3.6b's pose compile may refuse it). `weight` blends from the base pose: positions linearly, rotations by slerp.
3. **Splits.** `chest.rot` is shared 0.3/0.7 by `spine` and `chest` (the source's posture split); `head.rot` is shared 0.4/0.6 by `neck` and `head` (the source's head split). A head aim replaces `head.rot` (spec: "an `AimTarget` with `part: 'head'` takes a look-at instead").
4. **End rotations.** A foot or hand effector's `rot` is the end's world rotation in its `space`. Without one, a re-solved foot keeps its base world rotation (a planted foot stays flat while the hips move, as the source's `footTurn`); a hand keeps its local rotation (the source's reach and flinch pass no end turn).
5. **When a limb solves.** A foot solves when it has an effector position, a pin, or the pelvis effector moved the hips (then its target is its base world position: "the legs re-solve so the feet stay on their targets"). A hand solves when it has an effector position, a pin or a hand aim. A pin outranks an effector position; a hand aim outranks a hand effector position.
6. **Default poles** are the source's, in root space: knees `[0, 0, 1]` (forward), elbows `[x·0.7, −0.5, −0.5]` (back, down and out; `x` is +1 for the left side, −1 for the right). A `PoleTarget` gives the direction in its `space`.
7. **Aim.** A head aim turns the head's forward axis (+z) from the head joint toward the point; a chest aim the chest's. The angle still to go is measured as yaw and pitch in the pelvis's frame and accumulated; each joint's local rotation is its base rotation times `euler(share·pitch, share·yaw, 0)` (head: spine 0.1, chest 0.2, neck 0.3, head 0.4; chest: spine 0.3, chest 0.7), clamped by the limits after every step. It iterates (at most 16 steps, stopping when the angle left is under 1e-9 rad or stops shrinking), so a joint at its limit leaves the rest to the others, and a point past all of them leaves every joint at its limit. A hand aim points the arm straight from the shoulder at the point (the hand on the shoulder-to-point ray at full reach) and lines the hand up with the forearm. `report.reached.aim` is the worst aim's angle in degrees, `ok` within 0.5° (I-e).
8. **Limits, per joint.** Elbows and knees are hinges: the bend about the joint's local x (flexion: a knee bends back, an elbow forward), clamped to `[min, max]`. Shoulders and hips are cones: the bone's swing from hanging straight down, clamped to `cone`, keeping its twist. The chest's twist (yaw) and pitch ranges are shared by `spine` and `chest` by the posture split (the spine gets 0.3 of each bound, the chest 0.7), so the torso as a whole stays inside them; the neck clamps yaw and pitch, the head yaw, pitch and roll, each read as the source's euler of the joint's local rotation. Clamping rotates only past the bound back to it, so a joint inside its limits is never touched. `report.limited` lists every joint any clamp moved during the solve, in `JOINTS` order.
9. **Report units.** `error` is in figure units for positions (limbs, pins, pelvis) and in degrees for rotations (chest, head, a rotation-only pelvis) and aim. A position is `ok` within 1e-6 figure units, a rotation within 1e-6°, an aim within 0.5°.
10. **Tuned limits** (fixture `figure-rigs/default.toml`, M3.5a's content, values changed here; reasons in step 7's log): elbow and knee 0° to 150° (spec), shoulder cone 170° (an arm points almost straight up), hip cone 120° (a deep sit or a climb), chest twist ±35° (spec) and pitch −20° to 30°, neck yaw ±35° and pitch −20° to 25°, head yaw ±45°, pitch −25° to 30°, roll ±25°. The spec's "neck yaw ±70°, pitch −40° to +50°" is read as the head on the chest, now split over the neck and the head; the whole turn from the hips is at most 10.5 + 24.5 + 35 + 45 = 115°. They are starting values for the Studio's drags (EDS.9b).
11. **Cost.** The source's allocating `twoBoneIK` and `fk` are used as they are (S7-Q8: typed-array buffers only if it misses). A `bench` vitest project (as `packages/physics`) measures and prints, never asserts; it is not in `pnpm check`.

## Decisions (open to the Owner's veto)

1. **`FigureState`** is §4.4.5's, with four additions the order needs, each optional: `action.replaced` (the action this one replaced: `{ id, startedAt, length? }`), so the 80 ms pose-space transition is a pure function of the state (the outgoing clip is sampled at the replacement instant, `action.startedAt`, which is "the outgoing clip's last sampled handles"); `stances` as `{ id, startedAt, endedAt? }[]` instead of ids, since a stance's blend ramp in and out needs its start and its end; `pins` (a limb's world position, solved every frame, §4.4.5; GAME-3D.1g's ground contact uses it); and `hair` (the hair spring's swing, which the view steps with `hairStep`, as base() reads it). `speed` is kept and unused (the gait's cycle sets the walk's phase).
2. **`MotionLibrary`** is the spec's `Library`: `{ gaits, poses, clips, items, stands, rigs }`, each a record by id (`FigureLibrary` already names `build`'s). The figure's gait is `gaitOf(def, gaits)`, its rig limits `rigs[def.rig]` (else `DEFAULT_RIG_LIMITS`), its stand `stands[def.stand]` (else the gait's relaxed one), its item `items[state.item]` held in `def.hand`.
3. **The figure's space.** `evaluate` places the root at the origin facing `state.facing`; aim targets and pins are in that space (the view subtracts the figure's position, as its node carries it). Turning on the spot (the shown facing, the shuffle, the head's lead) stays the view's (M3.5d) and is not in this task.
4. **Options:** `evaluate(skeleton, def, state, time, library, options?)` with `options.previous` (the previous frame's time, so stepped and smooth sampling snap to a marker passed since: M3.6c's `stepTime` and `smoothTime`), `options.smooth` (sample clips every frame instead of at their rate) and `options.warn` with `options.warned` (a set the caller keeps): a clip id the library lacks warns once per id through `warn` and the figure keeps its locomotion (§4.4.5).
5. **Layers.** Locomotion (step 1) is `base()` with the stand's placed hands (`evaluateMotion` without the item). Each clip layer (stances in the sim's order, then the action) is sampled with the chest frame of the layers below (`chestFrame` of the locomotion's world) and **masked** (decision 6). It is merged over the layers below at its ramp weight `w`: a channel the layer names and a lower layer names blends with M3.6b's `blend` at `w`; a channel only it names comes in with its weight times `w` (`EffectorTarget.weight`, "blending from the base pose"); channels it does not name are left as they were. The merged pose goes through `applyPose` onto the locomotion pose, then `solve` with the state's aim and pins.
6. **Masks** own channels: `root` the root offset; `pelvis` the pelvis handle and fk `pelvis`; `torso` the chest handle and fk `spine`, `chest`; `head` the head handle and fk `neck`, `head`, `socket.*`, `hair.*`; `armL`/`armR` the hand handle, the elbow pole and fk `clavicle`, `upperArm`, `foreArm`, `hand`; `legL`/`legR` the foot handle, the knee pole and fk `thigh`, `shin`, `foot`, `hem`. A clip's channels outside its mask are dropped.
7. **The blend ramp:** `w = min(1, elapsed / inMs)` in; out over `outMs` from the clip's end (a `once` clip's end is its length, holding its last pose while it fades; a stance's is `endedAt`; `loop`, `pingpong` and `hold` actions do not end by themselves); 0 ms is a cut. A layer at `w = 0` is not active.
8. **Transitions** (§4.4.5 step 3, M3R-D16): with `action.replaced`, the outgoing clip's masked sample at `action.startedAt` (its own ramp applied) and the incoming's masked sample are blended with `blend(outgoing, incoming, u)`, `u = (time − startedAt) / inMs` (the new clip's `blend.inMs`, 80 ms when it gives none, 0 a cut); a channel only one owns fades against the layers below (`blend`'s rule). During the transition the incoming clip's own ramp in is not applied again. One pose blend per transition.
9. **The additive mode** adds rotations only: an additive clip's fk turns and its pelvis, chest and head turns (split as the solver splits them: chest 0.3/0.7 to spine and chest, head 0.4/0.6 to neck and head) multiply the merged pose's fk at its weight. Its positions, limb handles and poles are ignored (an additive offset of a hand target has no single meaning over an IK layer); a later compile warning for them is a follow-up.
10. **Aim** (step 4) is the state's `aim` passed to `solve`, every frame, over every clip. The spec's "unless a clip declares the part locked" has no field in M3.6c's clip schema, so no part is locked; adding one is an additive schema field (follow-up).
11. **Holds** (step 6): the state's item is applied with `applyHold` after the solve only to an arm no active layer owns. The stand's placed hands belong to the locomotion layer, so a clip owning that arm replaces them through its handles.
12. **Secondary** (step 7): when a layer or a pin moved the pelvis or a leg, the hems are re-placed on the solved legs (`placeHems`; walking, on the line of travel). The hair takes the state's swing in step 1. The re-skin is the view's.
13. **F-g's reference state and measure.** FigureBuilder's actions run on Wick's body (the fixture sliders) in its gait (`bounce`), standing at t 0, facing +z, the right hand for `reach`. The end poses: `reach` at its hold (half its duration), `look` at its held end (t 4 s) and its out pose (t 1 s), `flinch` held (t 1 s). The measure: every joint's world position in figure units. The fixture clips reproduce FigureBuilder's turns as fk turns: the chest's are FigureBuilder's own (it multiplies the chest's local turn); the neck's and head's are written as the local turn equal to FigureBuilder's change of `headYaw` and `headPitch` at that state (FigureBuilder turns them inside the joint's Euler angles, which no local turn equals on every body; on the keeper the same clip lands within 6e-3, on its hair pivots, recorded in the log). The poses keep 4 decimals (the canonical spelling), within 5e-5 figure units. The clips' numbers come from the committed generator (`scripts/figurebuilder-action-goldens.ts`), which also writes the goldens.
14. **Fixture clips:** `reach` (length 1.4 s, the gait's `reach`; `once`; mask `armR`, `torso`; keys at 0, 0.49, 1.008 and 1.4: FigureBuilder's 0.35 and 0.72 of its envelope), `look` (3.4 s; `hold`; mask `torso`, `head`; keys at 0, 0.6, 1.6, 3.4) and `flinch` (0.18 s; `hold`; mask `pelvis`, `torso`, `head`, `armL`, `armR`, `legR`; keys at 0 and 0.18), with the poses they name (`reach-out`, `look-out`, `look-across`, `flinch`) and a `slash` and `roll` for I-k (from the fixture `jab` and `guard-high`). Tests and examples only.

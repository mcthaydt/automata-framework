## Decisions (open to the Owner's veto)

1. **No `id` field.** As `PoseDef` and `FigureDef`, a clip's id is its file name (`figure-clips/<id>.toml`).
2. **Eases.** A key's `ease` shapes the segment that ends at it ("ease into this key"); `linear` when left out, and the first key's ease is never used. `step` holds the previous key until this one's time, then jumps. `in`, `out` and `inOut` are CSS's `ease-in` (0.42, 0, 1, 1), `ease-out` (0, 0, 0.58, 1) and `ease-in-out` (0.42, 0, 0.58, 1); a tuple is a CSS `cubic-bezier(x1, y1, x2, y2)` with `x1` and `x2` in [0, 1] (so time never runs backwards) and `y` free (overshoot is allowed). The curve is solved for its x by Newton's method with a bisection fallback, to 1e-9.
3. **Sampling.** `sample(clip, t, poses, context?)` returns the pose at clip time `t` (clamped to `[0, length]`): the key at or before `t` and the next, blended with M3.6b's `blend` at the eased fraction. A key's `pose` is a pose id (looked up in `poses`, the `figure-pose` content; a missing one throws a `RangeError` naming it, which content compile already prevents) or a pose written inline. Keys hold handles, not joint angles; the caller solves the result.
4. **Keys in different spaces, and the chest frame.** When a channel (an effector or a pole) is in `chest` space at one key and `root` at the next (a pole one key leaves out counts as its default, in root space), both sides of that channel are converted to `root` space before `blend`, which refuses mixed spaces (M3.6b decision 7): a position through the chest frame, a hand or foot `rot` by the chest's turn, a pole direction by the chest's turn. The chest frame is an **input**: `context.chest`, the chest joint's frame in root space with its translation in figure units (an `M`), from the pose evaluated so far (§4.4.5 steps 1 and 2, before this clip). `chestFrame(skeleton, world)` makes it from a solved world. A mixed pair sampled with no `context.chest` throws a `RangeError` naming the channel, so a caller cannot silently get a frame-less answer. Channels whose spaces agree are left in their space (a chest-space carry stays with the chest).
5. **The compile warning on mixed spaces.** The content compiler has no way for a kind's schema to add a warning (its only warning is `skippedFile`), and a key naming a pose by id needs the pose library, which a schema of one item cannot see. So the check is a function, `clipWarnings(clip, poses)`, listing each pair of neighbouring keys whose channel changes space, with the key index and the channel; it is importable (§4.4.6, "the compile checks of §6, importable as functions") and the fixture test runs it over the compiled content. Wiring it into `automata content` as a compile warning needs a warning channel in `@mcthaydt/automata-content` (a content-package change, outside this lane); it is recorded as a gap for the PR body.
6. **Loop modes** are `clipTime(clip, elapsed)`: the clip time for `elapsed` seconds of play. `once` and `hold` clamp to `[0, length]` (`evaluate` decides that `once` has ended and `hold` stays); `loop` wraps (`length` itself is 0, so a loop's end is its start); `pingpong` runs forward then back over `2·length`. A negative `elapsed` is 0.
7. **Stepped sampling** is `stepTime(clip, t, rate)`: `t` floored to the pose step (`rate` steps a second: the clip's `rate`, else the gait's `poseRate`, which the caller passes), except that a step containing a marker shows the marker's instant from that instant on (the latest marker in the step at or before `t`). So the `contact` pose shows on the first frame at or after the marker, never before it and never a step late (§4.4.4). Smooth sampling passes `t` itself.
8. **`retime(clip, length)`** scales key times, markers and cues by `length / clip.length` (the last key lands on `length` exactly); a length that is not a positive finite number throws a `RangeError`. **`markerTime(clip, name)`** returns the marker's time and throws a `RangeError` naming a missing one (a sim action naming a missing marker is the game's content check, M3.6d).
9. **Compile checks** (§6; I-j's clip half), each a content compile error at its location: keys not strictly increasing in time (two keys at one time would be a zero-length segment; a cut is two keys a pose step apart), the first key not at 0 or the last not at `length`, fewer than two keys, a `mask` group not in `root, pelvis, torso, head, armL, armR, legL, legR`, a marker or cue outside `[0, length]`, a key naming a missing pose (`contentRef('figure-pose')`, so the compiler reports the file, line and column), an inline pose failing the pose checks, a `length` or `rate` that is not positive, a blend ramp below 0 ms. `mask` must be non-empty and its groups distinct.
10. **Out of scope** (M3.6d's `evaluate`): what `mask`, `mode = "additive"` and the `blend` ramp do to the layers below, transitions between actions, the stretch warning (0.5× to 2×, which needs the sim's action lengths) and the fixture clips `reach`, `look` and `flinch`. This task defines and checks those fields only.

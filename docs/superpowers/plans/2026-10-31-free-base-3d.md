## 1. What the free game is (from the rescope §2, decided here to the file level)

Emberwick's shape in 3D, one verb, no combat:

- **Flow and screens:** splash → menu (New, Continue, Settings) → play → pause (Resume, Settings, Quit) → end (goal
  reached). Same screens and copy pattern as `games/emberwick/src/screens`, through `defaultScreens` /
  `@mcthaydt/automata-game`; the Settings screen has the core sections plus the camera speed and invert (M4.12's rows);
  **no** Lock-mode section, no Items screen, no Map screen.
- **Saves:** one slot via `Hosting`: `{ embers, goalReached }`, its own `schema` id (`base-3d-free`), version 1; a stale
  adventure save is ignored, not read (rescope §4). A denied or missing hosted capability shows the hosted failure and
  never falls back to device storage (F3-d; adapter tests as Emberwick's).
- **The verb: the roll.** Walking cannot cross a channel or pit; the roll's lunge does. The roll already exists in
  `systems/hero.ts` (#342: `ACTION_BITS.action`, its speed, distance and timing as `HERO_MOVES`); the free game keeps that
  one move and drops the rest of `HERO_MOVES` (slash, point, the chain). Distance and speed are tuning (§4.3).
- **The room:** one `af-sector/1` file, `src/content/levels/room.af-sector.json`, authored in ED3D: a start platform,
  two ledges, one or two channels and a pit the roll crosses and walking does not, ember pickups on both sides of them,
  the goal ember last; sector `view` ids and `occlude` walls as the camera needs; a fall puts Wick on the last safe spot
  with no loss (the fall-and-recover code from #342: `Location.return*`, kept). Read by the converter that #354 adds
  (`content/levels/sector-area.ts`, kept and trimmed: the free room has no `foe`, no dungeon pieces, one `area-exit`-free
  area; `ember` entities become the pickups, one `goal`).
- **The hero:** Wick as the procedural figure (content already in `src/content/figure*`: keep Wick, the IK rig, the poses
  and the idle, walk and roll clips; delete the others), the third-person controller (camera-relative, linear ramp,
  M3R-D15 latch, heading in the sim: `hero.ts`'s movement and `CameraHeading`), the angled free-turn camera.
- **HUD:** ember count and Pause only (Emberwick's pattern); touch: the stick, the camera drag and **one** roll button
  (48 px, `required`, the `end` group); keys WASD / arrows move, Q/E and the right-button drag turn the camera, Space or K
  rolls; pad: left stick, right stick, A rolls, Start pauses; 44 px touch targets; the landscape rotate gate as the 2D game
  decides for phones.
- **Out:** lock-on, combat, hurt, health and hearts, enemies, the boss, weapons, shield, sling, bombs, hookchain, keys,
  doors, switches, blocks, chests, pots, cracked walls, the map and inventory screens, ground-contact IK, cutscenes.

## Scope

In (`packages/feel`, presentation only: no sim entry, no `boundaries` row):
- **Content:** the `feel` kind, `feel.toml`: `[limits]` (`shake_px`, `hit_stop_ms`, `flash_frames`, `flash_alpha`, defaults from the spec),
  `[springs]` (squash), and `[[entry]]` rows with `on` (a sim event id or a `*` pattern) and the channels `camera`, `pose`, `visual`,
  `word`, `haptic`, `audio`. `limitWarnings` names every value above a limit (the runtime clamps it too).
- **Dynamics:** `SecondOrder` and `snapTo`, `SquashBook`, `LeanBook`, `HitStop`, `Beats` (events held until the drawn tick reaches them,
  each once, a stale one dropped), hashed noise and the trauma shake: the arcade source's, generic over the view key, with their tests.
- **`createFeel`:** `hear(tick, events)` and `frame(drawnTick, alpha, frameSeconds)` giving a `FeelFrame`: whole-pixel `offset`,
  `frozen` views, `flash`, `tint`, `squash` on 1/32 steps, `words`, and the `haptics` and `cues` that fired this frame as data.
  `frameNow(drawnTick, alpha)` is the same under Effect's `Clock` (`TestClock` in tests). Settings (`reduceFlashing`, `haptics`,
  `intensity`) and reduced motion are read live from functions the game passes; content is a getter, so a reload is read next fire.
- **`feelExtension`:** the content kind (the renderer channels, haptics port and FeelSection join it in M6.1b and M6.1c).
- **F-a, F-b, F-c** as tests (F-b's sim hash against a run without `feel`).

Out: the Pixi layers and `camera2d.shake` call (M6.1b), `HapticsPort`, FeelSection, hot-reload tuning, `pipe feel coverage`, the
FloorFifty preset (M6.1c), the arcade source's dust pool and word pool (they draw: M6.1b).

## Decisions for the Owner's veto

- **FE-D1:** an event reaches `feel` as `hear(tick, events)`: the runtime's `sim.events` fact carries no tick, so the game's loop (or
  M6.1b's binding) passes the step's tick. Default: no change to core; a request to publish the tick is a follow-up.
- **FE-D2:** `frame` takes the frame's seconds, and `frameNow` reads them from `Clock`; feel keeps its own clock as their sum, so a
  frame is a pure function of the events, the drawn tick, the settings and the frame times.
- **FE-D3:** `intensity` scales camera and pose effects (shake, hit-stop length, squash), not flash, words or haptics.
- **FE-D4:** the arcade source's racer-specific names (`PlayerId`, `JuiceTuning`) become a generic view key and a plain tuning object.

The touch move stick's ring stays on screen at a compact height (780 x 270). At rest it stands on the zone's foot instead of running past the screen's edge below it, and held it is drawn as near the thumb as the visible region allows, clear of the safe area and Ruken's reserved strip. The stick still floats: its vector runs from where the thumb landed, and the ring, the zone and the touch targets keep their sizes.

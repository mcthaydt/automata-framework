The runtime's own words follow the language in force: the flow's (a slow load, a failed download), the display policy's (the landscape gate, the rotate hint and toast) and the lost-picture and boot-failure panels are chosen from the saved choice, the first-run pick or the host's language, then the browser's, and change when the language changes, where they were fixed at boot from the browser alone. `defineGame`'s `copy` may be a function of the locale, so a game can reword the runtime's words in each language.

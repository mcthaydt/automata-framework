On a phone held sideways, the touch buttons now stand in the empty bars beside the level instead of over it. Where a bar is too narrow to hold a button, where a column would be taller than the screen (a large text size) or where a stick is drawn, the row along the foot stays. The renderer publishes the bar widths as `--af-bar-left` and `--af-bar-right`.
